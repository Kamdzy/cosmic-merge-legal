Cosmic Merge Privacy Policy

Developer: Tehox Studios · Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Cosmic Merge (Android package com.tehoxstudios.cosmicmerge) collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: the game stores your progress, lets you sign in with Google Play Games to keep it across devices, and shows optional ads from Google AdMob. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (scores, achievements, settings, Stardust balance, purchases you own) is saved in a file on your device. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

2. Information processed by services the game uses

Google Play Games Services (Google)Your Play Games player ID and profile name, achievement unlocks, leaderboard scoresSigning in, achievements and leaderboards. Scores you submit can be visible to other players on leaderboards.

These companies process data under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Ads and your choices

  • Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy in the game.
  • If you do not consent, ads may still appear but are not personalized.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose to watch them in exchange for an in-game reward.

4. Children

Cosmic Merge is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Cloud-saved progress is kept for as long as your game account exists. To have your cloud save and game account deleted, email us at the address below with your request. Uninstalling the game deletes the progress stored on your device. Leaderboard and achievement data held by Google Play Games can be managed in your Google account and the Google Play Games app.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA/UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Data sent between the game and the services above is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we use the protections these established providers offer.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
